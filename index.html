Which dumpster size should I choose?

A 10-yard dumpster works well for smaller cleanouts and renovations. A 15-yard dumpster provides more capacity for larger household, commercial, and construction projects.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

L&L Dumpsters offers flexible 1-day, 3-day, and 7-day rental periods based on your project timeline.

Can I rent a dumpster any day of the week?

Yes, you can schedule a dumpster rental any day of the week. However, Friday rentals require a minimum 3-day rental period, and weekend service incurs additional fees.

What materials can I place in the dumpster?

Accepted materials include household junk, furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, yard waste, and construction debris. Contact us before loading anything uncertain.

Do you provide same-day dumpster delivery?

Same-day delivery may be available depending on your location, dumpster availability, and booking time. Contact us to confirm availability.

What items are not accepted?

Concrete, dirt, bricks, paint, chemicals, batteries, and tires are not accepted. Ask our team if you are unsure about a specific item.
